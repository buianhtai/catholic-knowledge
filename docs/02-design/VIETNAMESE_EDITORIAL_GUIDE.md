# Vietnamese editorial guide

Use natural Catholic Vietnamese rather than literal translation. Prefer established Church terms and consistent transliteration.

## Preferred terms
- Jesus: Chúa Giêsu
- Christ: Đức Kitô / Chúa Kitô
- Church: Giáo Hội
- Council of Nicaea: Công đồng Nixêa
- Constantine: Constantinô
- Saint Augustine: Thánh Augustinô
- Saint Ambrose: Thánh Ambrôsiô
- Confessions: Tự Thuật
- Grace: Ân sủng
- Pentecost: Lễ Ngũ Tuần
- Magisterium: Huấn quyền
- Sacred Tradition: Thánh Truyền

## Style
- Prefer concise, idiomatic Vietnamese over word-for-word English structures.
- Capitalize Church-specific proper names consistently.
- For liturgical commemorations, name the celebration rather than only the saint when appropriate.
- Keep historical names/transliterations consistent across navigation, journeys, and entity pages.
