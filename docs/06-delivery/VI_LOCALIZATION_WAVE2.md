# Vietnamese-first localization — wave 2

This wave completes the visible Vietnamese-first pass across the main product surfaces.

## Included
- Vietnamese route aliases for primary navigation and content pages.
- Vietnamese Home links use Vietnamese slugs.
- High-contrast text overlays over editorial imagery.
- Vietnamese content for Church History, Scripture, Nicaea, Liturgy, Learning Journey, Ask, Kids Mode, Places and Augustine Explorer.
- Real sourced La Vang photography with attribution metadata.
- Image-first cards replace remaining icon-first content on key pages.

## Readability rule
Text placed over photography or artwork must sit on a high-contrast surface (solid or sufficiently opaque overlay) and must not rely on image tone for readability.
